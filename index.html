Problem

  • The first challenge will be breaking into the market, given the presence of two major industry giants: Nike and Adidas.
  • We will be the first to implement this idea in Egypt and the Middle East.

    • The Solution or What we're going to try to do...

  • The first thing we’ll do is turn to the audience, because without them, we won’t succeed or even be able to establish ourselves.
  • We will start with a small number of products, and we will focus on quality and design.
  • We will work on building a strong community around the brand, and we will listen to their feedback and suggestions.
  • We will create a unique brand identity that reflects our values and mission.
  • We will differentiate ourselves from the competition by offering high-quality products at a reasonable price.
  • We will focus on building strong relationships with our customers and creating a loyal customer base.
  • We will leverage social media and digital marketing to reach a wider audience and increase brand awareness.

Plan

Our Plan

Phase 1: Brand Establishment

  • Finalize brand identity and logo design.
  • We are the start that attracts people to us, and we make people like the brand.
  • Build a strong online presence and community.
  • Engage with customers and gather feedback.

Phase 2: TEASE

  • We start posting content and teasers for the products.

Phase 3: Product Launch

  • The first collection of sportswear products will be launched.
  • They are the ones I'll start with, and they'll be in stores and online markets as a start to building customers—they really liked the brand until we reach the sales target.
  • Engage with influencers and athletes to promote the products.
  • After that, we’ll develop ourselves more and be more present in the market, and I’ll actually start releasing a collection of sports shoes of all kinds.
  • After the big drop in prices, I'll start with something that might be a bit new, like the rest of the brand, that we'll make a course for all sports.
  • After this, we can go ahead and start opening actual stores from here.
  • Open physical stores in key locations.

Phase 4: Expansion & Growth

  • We'll start with the basic idea, which is that we participate in the local leagues and look for the first club we can sign with.
  • We’ll try to choose a good club from the smaller clubs so we can build on it.
  • We’ll focus on clubs in the lower divisions (third and fourth).
  • We’ll work on building a strong team and brand presence in the local leagues.
  • After a lot of research on clubs that could work with us and that we could sign contracts with, and they’re the ones we’ll be able to grow ourselves with.
  • Like Ismaily Club, Wadi Degla team, Mansoura team, Canal team, and Railway team
  • We’ll work on building a strong team and brand presence in the local leagues.
  • And after that, we'll start going out a bit in the outdoor courses.
